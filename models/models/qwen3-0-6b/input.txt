Paris is the capital and largest city of France. It is located on the Seine River, in the north of the country, at the heart of the Ile-de-France region. The city has been one of the major centres of finance, diplomacy, commerce, culture, fashion and gastronomy for centuries. Its museums, monuments and churches attract millions of visitors every year, and its metro and railway network connects it with the rest of Europe.
